Start Atlas

Atlas Web downloads a small AI model (Qwen 2.5, 0.5B, about 500 MB) once from Hugging Face, the model's own host. After that it runs on this device and your words never leave it. The download is cached, so next time it starts much faster.

          About Atlas Web

          Atlas AI is a free, space-themed AI assistant for Android made by Taha Armin. It has chat, four AI personalities, response length settings, voice chat, a "Hey Atlas" voice assistant and an AI keyboard.

          I rebuilt the parts that make sense on the web, so it works with VoiceOver, TalkBack and a keyboard in any modern browser. The original app sends your messages to a cloud AI service. Atlas Web runs a small open model right on your device instead, so it is private and needs no keys. The tradeoff is that answers are simpler and slower than a big cloud model.

          The four personalities (Atlas, Hyperion, Astraea and Prometheus) and the response lengths come from the original app. All of them use the same on-device model here.

          Not included: the "Hey Atlas" always-on wake word, the quick settings tile and the system keyboard, because browsers cannot do those.
